Frequently asked

How do I get whitelisted?+

Enter your X handle, complete the follow/like/repost/comment tasks, then submit your Gmail and wallet address. Approved applicants are notified before the private mint window opens.

Can I use the same wallet twice?+

No. Each wallet address can hold one whitelist spot, and duplicate submissions are filtered out during review.

What happens if I miss the whitelist window?+

You can still mint during the public sale at 0.003 ETH, as long as supply remains.
